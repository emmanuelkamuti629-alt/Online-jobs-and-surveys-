Help & Support
Frequently Asked
Why haven't I received payment?
Payments go through: Pending → Under Review → Approved → Paid. If your task is still Pending, an admin has not yet confirmed it.
How do withdrawals work?
You can withdraw once your Available balance ≥ KES 200. Your first withdrawal requires a one-time KES 499 activation fee.
Why was my task rejected?
Tasks can be rejected if the answers are incomplete or contradictory. You'll see the reason in your History tab.
How do referrals work?
Share your referral link with friends. When they sign up and complete their first paid task, you earn a reward.
